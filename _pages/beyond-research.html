“

Broad, wholesome, charitable views of men and things cannot be acquired by vegetating in one little corner of the earth all one’s lifetime.

Mark Twain, The Innocents Abroad

Curiosity does not stop at the edge of a research problem. Traveling, hiking, cooking, and sports place me in unfamiliar settings and encourage me to observe, adapt, listen, and learn. They broaden my understanding of how people live, what they value, and how differently the same challenge can be experienced.

That perspective also shapes the questions I ask as a researcher. Technical rigor matters, but so do the people affected by a decision. Exploring the world helps me question assumptions, approach problems with humility, and pursue work that creates a thoughtful and lasting impact.
