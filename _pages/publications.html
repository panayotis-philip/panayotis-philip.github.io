My work develops optimization methods for decision-making under uncertainty, with applications in inventory, machine learning, and high-dimensional contextual problems.

ManuscriptSubmitted for journal publication

A Distributionally Robust Optimization Approach to Quick Response Models under Demand Uncertainty

Panayotis P. Papavassilopoulos, Grani A. Hanasusanto, and Yijie Wang

We formulate and solve a distributionally robust inventory model that accounts for ambiguity in demand distributions. The work derives tractable optimization reformulations and evaluates them through large-scale CVXPY experiments, demonstrating reductions in waste-to-consumption ratios across uncertainty scenarios.

Conference paperICSP 2025 · Paris, France

Data-Driven Distributionally Robust Contextual Optimization with Gaussian Mixture Models

Y. C. Yoon, P. Papavassilopoulos, G. A. Hanasusanto, and Yijie Wang

We develop a contextual stochastic optimization framework using Gaussian mixture models and distributionally robust methods. The approach achieves strong sample complexity and out-of-sample performance across high-dimensional decision problems.

Accepted and presented at the XVII International Conference on Stochastic Programming, July 31, 2025.

Master's thesisNTUA · 2023

Assessment of Sustainable Development Goals with Integrated Assessment Models in the Energy Field

Panayotis P. Papavassilopoulos · Written in Greek

I developed a method to estimate minimum objective-function ranges in multi-objective optimization, improving robustness and computational efficiency. I applied the method to determine investment policies for renewable energy sources.
